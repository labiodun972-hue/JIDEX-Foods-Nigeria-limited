ABOUT JIDEX FOODS

Building a Stronger Future Through Local Food Manufacturing

JIDEX Foods Nigeria Limited was founded by Olajide Abubakar Rasak with a vision of transforming digital success into sustainable manufacturing and creating affordable, high-quality food products for Nigerian families.

From its home in Ijebu-Ode, Ogun State, JIDEX Foods has grown into a modern food company producing quality snacks while creating employment, supporting local agriculture and expanding its distribution network across Nigeria.

FOUNDER & CEO

Olajide Abubakar Rasak

Founder & CEO, JIDEX Foods Nigeria Limited

Olajide Abubakar Rasak, popularly known as Jidex, is a Nigerian entrepreneur, digital creator and visionary business leader.

He founded JIDEX Foods Nigeria Limited with the vision of transforming digital influence into sustainable manufacturing, creating employment opportunities, promoting local production and delivering quality food products that are affordable for every Nigerian family.

Today, JIDEX Foods continues to expand through innovation, quality production and a commitment to empowering the next generation of entrepreneurs.

OUR JOURNEY

Learning, Growing & Building Excellence

Before expanding JIDEX Foods Nigeria Limited, our Founder travelled across Nigeria to gain practical knowledge of modern food manufacturing, production processes and quality standards.

This commitment to continuous learning became the foundation upon which JIDEX Foods was built, helping transform a vision into a modern food production company serving customers with quality products.
